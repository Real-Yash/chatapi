Chat Widget Demo

This page embeds the chat widget for agent demo-bakery. Look for the floating bubble in the bottom-right corner — click it to open the chat panel (http://localhost:3000/c/demo-bakery?embed=true).

Prerequisites: backend on :8000, frontend on :3000.

<script src="./widget.js"
  data-agent="demo-bakery"
  data-api="http://localhost:8000"
  data-base="http://localhost:3000"></script>
